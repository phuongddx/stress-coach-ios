# Ripple v2 app icon: Apple HIG check

Checked against the [Apple HIG app icons page](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons), using the checklist from PR #86 (`design/app-icon/b-surfacing/HIG-CHECK.md`). Checked on 2026-11-24.

Sources checked: `StressMonitor/StressMonitor/AppIcon.icon/icon.json` and its SVG layers, `StressMonitor/StressMonitorWatch Watch App/AppIcon.icon/icon.json`, the design renders in `ui-v2-design/ripple-v2/icon/render/` (Default, Dark, ClearLight, ClearDark, TintedLight, TintedDark, Watch), `design/ripple-v2/icon/ripple-light-1024.png`, and `actool` output (Xcode 27.1).

| # | HIG point | Ripple v2 | HIG section |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Use the layered Icon Composer format (one background, one or more foreground layers) | ✅ `AppIcon.icon` has 2 groups (Face, Hood), one SVG layer each. The background is an Icon Composer linear-gradient fill (green `#3E9A86` → `#1F6A5C`). `actool` compiles it to an `IconImageStack` with `AppIcon/Face` and `AppIcon/Hood` groups. | [Icon formats](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons#Icon-formats) · [Layer design](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons#Layer-design) |
| 2 | Clear edges on foreground layers, vector art preferred | ✅ SVG layers (1024 viewBox) with hard-edged filled shapes. No raster images in the layers. | [Layer design](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons#Layer-design) |
| 3 | A background that stands out and puts the foreground first | ✅ Mid/deep green gradient background. The pale mint hood and light face sit on top of it. About 11% of the light master is near-white (L > 0.80), all of it inside the face/hood, none on the background. | [Layer design](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons#Layer-design) |
| 4 | Default, dark, clear and tinted appearances | ✅ Light, dark and tinted art per layer and per background fill in `icon.json`. The system makes clear. The design folder has Default, Dark, ClearLight, ClearDark, TintedLight and TintedDark renders. | 5 | Same core features in every variant, dark built from light | ✅ The dark face and hood SVGs are byte-identical to the light ones; only the background changes (near-black green `#16352D` → `#0B1411`). The tinted SVGs are a grayscale version of the same shapes. | [Appearances](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons#Appearances) |
| 6 | No text | ✅ No `<text>` in any layer | [Design](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons#Design) |
| 7 | Illustration, not photo | ✅ Flat vector illustration | [Design](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons#Design) |
| 8 | Square, unmasked layers, no rounded corners in the art | ✅ Square 1024 canvas, full-bleed background fill, no mask in the art. The light master PNG is square and full-bleed. | [Icon shape](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons#Icon-shape) |
| 9 | No baked-in specular highlights, drop shadows, bevels, blurs or glows | ✅ No filters, blurs or gradients in the SVG layers. Shadow (`neutral`, 0.5) and specular are Icon Composer group settings that the system draws. The only in-art "shading" is a flat darker crescent on the hood's lower right. | [Visual effects](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons#Visual-effects) |
| 10 | Keep it simple: few shapes, no very thin lines | ⚠️ Few shapes (hood, ears, face, hair, eyes, cheeks, smile). The thinnest line is the smile: stroke 6 × scale 2.3 ≈ 14 px at 1024, so about 0.5 px at 40 px. | [Design](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons#Design) |
| 11 | Still reads small: 40 px, 29 pt Settings (58/87 px) | ✅ Checked by eye on the regenerated 40, 58 and 87 px fallbacks: hood, ears, hair, eyes and cheeks read at 40 px. The smile is faint at 40 px and clear at 58 and 87 px. | [Design](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons#Design) · [Specifications](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons#Specifications) |
| 12 | iOS 1024×1024 master | ✅ `ripple-light-1024.png` is 1024×1024 and the layers use a 1024 viewBox | [Specifications](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons#Specifications) |
| 13 | watchOS: square layers, circular mask, 1088×1088 canvas, content centered | ✅ The watch `AppIcon.icon` uses the same layers with `supported-platforms: circles: [watchOS]`. On the 1024 square master the farthest ear point is about 410 px from the center, inside the 512 px circle, so the square fallback also survives the watch mask. Checked against the circle, not against Apple's template grid. | [Icon shape](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons#Icon-shape) · [Specifications](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons#Specifications) |

| 15 | Same design on every platform | ✅ iPhone and Watch bundles share byte-identical face and hood SVGs and the same background fill. The widget fallback uses the same PNG master. | [Design](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons#Design) |
| 16 | Opaque full-bleed bitmaps (iOS 18 / watchOS 11 fallback) | ✅ All 37 fallback PNGs (18 iPhone, 18 widget, 1 watch) are RGB with no alpha channel, made by `scripts/generate_app_icons.py` from `ripple-light-1024.png` | [Layer design](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons#Layer-design) |

Notes
- Fallback PNGs are the light appearance only. The appiconsets have no dark or tinted PNGs, so on iOS 18 / watchOS 11 the system shows the light icon in every mode (unchanged from before).
- Not verified: Home Screen appearance on a device or simulator. Verification was `actool` + `assetutil --info` only.
- As in #86: the HIG page lists no point sizes (40 px, 29 pt) and no numeric watchOS safe area, and it has no general "no transparency" rule. Icon Composer layers are transparent by design; only the background and the bitmap fallbacks must be opaque.
